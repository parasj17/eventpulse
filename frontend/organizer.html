Floor plan

Gates

Status is computed from live queue and exit load. Taking a gate out of service is for things like maintenance, not for overriding the live reading. At least 4 gates must stay working.

EventPulse AI model

A base model aligned through Nugen on EventPulse's own operations: its handbook and more than a hundred worked situations from this system's engines. It writes the operator plans and attendee messages across the app. Every answer is checked against the situation it was given; anything it can't back up is replaced by the playbook.

Ask the copilot

Ask in plain words, for example "which zone is worst right now?" or "what if the match ends late and one gate closes?". It reads the live model and runs the same what-if simulations as below. It cannot change anything: gates stay in your hands.

What-if simulator

Test a disruption before it happens. Nothing here changes the live venue: each run works on a copy of the data. Results use the hour set at the top of this page.

Field reports for the weather twin

Tell the weather twin what your team is seeing. A flooded street raises that zone's flood estimate straight away (the report fades over six hours); a measured transport load teaches the model how much rain really changes demand. Both are kept, so the model keeps what it learned. Open the weather twin

Partner access

For the platform that sells your tickets. Give its developers a key and they can list your events, read live conditions, and create each ticket buyer's personal live link to put in their own ticket email or app. A key reaches only the events at your venues. Developer guide
